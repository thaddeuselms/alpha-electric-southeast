## Image edit record

On August 28, 2026, `wine-room-cabinet-lighting.jpeg` was edited with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool to remove visible dirt, debris, stains, and scuffs from only the concrete floor. The electrical distribution cabinets, breakers, disconnects, conduits, walls, equipment-room structure, camera angle, and overall composition were held as invariants. The final JPEG was exported at the source dimensions of 1369 × 918 pixels. The filename is legacy metadata; the visible scene is an electrical equipment room rather than wine-room cabinetry.
